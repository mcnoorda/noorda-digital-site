You're doing the work of five people.

If you're running a roofing, siding, or gutter company, you're not just doing the work — you're the salesperson, the scheduler, the quote guy, and the collections department. Here's where it usually breaks down:

The lead that got away

A lead comes in while you're on a roof. By the time you call back that evening, they've already booked the guy who answered first.

Quotes at 9pm

You're building quotes at 9pm. Measurements on a notepad, plugged into a spreadsheet, sent the next afternoon — if you remember.

Jobs that go cold

Following up feels like nagging, so you don't. Good jobs quietly go cold because nobody circled back.

"What's the address again?"

Your crew texts "what's the address again?" for the third time today. You're the only one who knows the whole schedule.

Playing dispatcher

Customers call asking where the crew is. You stop what you're doing to play dispatcher.

Waiting on the check

You finish the job — then wait a month to get paid. Because chasing the invoice is one more thing you didn't get to.

None of this means you're bad at running your business. It means you're doing the work of five people with the tools of one. That's the part we fix.

Not at all. We build everything to run quietly in the background of the tools you already use. You don't learn new software or change how you work — the busywork just starts handling itself. If you can text a customer, you can use what we build.

What's this going to cost me?

The first call — the Workflow Audit — is free, and you walk away with a clear plan whether you hire us or not. If you move forward, we scope the build to the time and money it saves you. Most of what we build pays for itself inside the first few months. You'll know the number before you commit to anything.

What if it doesn't work for my business?

That's exactly what the free audit is for. We don't build anything until we've seen how your business actually runs and agreed it's worth doing. You see it working on your own jobs before it goes live. No long contracts, no ripping out what already works.
